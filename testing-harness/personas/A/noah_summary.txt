The interview is conducted in English. The interviewee, A, is a Process Coordinator responsible for optimizing and coordinating processes related to student ticket handling, complaint management, and incident resolution. A confirmed that this is the full list of main areas they handle.

For student ticket handling, the process begins when a student opens a ticket in the Sipac system, which takes about 15 minutes. The CRA team then opens the process in Sipac for further action. The coordination team checks for any pending issues, a step that takes around 2 hours. If there are pending issues, the student is notified via email, which takes about 10 minutes. Details are then entered into the Academic System, taking about 20 minutes, and the Approval Team is notified via email. The main bottleneck is incorrect ticket information, which requires the student to resubmit the correct details, causing delays that affect the student's timeline. Once the correct information is resubmitted, the ticket is updated in Sipac, and the process continues.

In complaint management, a complaint is received in the Complaint System, taking about 15 minutes, and a case is opened in the case management software. The complaint is then forwarded internally for verification references. Missing reference documents can delay verification, and if documents are unavailable, external sources are contacted, and alternative verification methods are coordinated with relevant teams. Once documents are obtained, the record keeper updates records in the verification system, and the complainant is informed via email. A confirmed this flow is complete.
