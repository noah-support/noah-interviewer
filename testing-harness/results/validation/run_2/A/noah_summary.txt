The interview is conducted in English. The interviewee, A, is a Process Coordinator focusing on process optimization and coordination, specifically handling student ticket handling, complaint management, and incident resolution. A confirmed that these are the main responsibilities and that the list is complete.

For student ticket handling, the process begins with a student opening a ticket in Sipac, taking about 15 minutes. The ticket is then handled by the CRA team, which takes about 30 minutes. A checks for any pending issues for another 30 minutes and forwards it for further checking in Sipac. The coordination team then emails the student about any pending issues. The main issue is incorrect ticket information, which delays processing until corrected by the student.

In complaint management, a complaint is received in the Complaint System, taking about 15 minutes, and then a case manager opens a new case. It is forwarded internally to the external team using an internal communication tool, taking about 20 minutes. References are gathered from the reference database, taking about an hour, and then passed to the verification team. The verification team confirms the references using the verification system, taking about 45 minutes, before it goes to the record keeper. A informs the complainant about the outcome via email, which takes about 10 minutes. Missing reference documents are a common issue, delaying verification until A contacts external sources.

In incident resolution, an incident is opened in the Incident Management System, taking about 10 minutes, and assigned to the resolution team, taking around 15 minutes. The resolution team updates the operator using a communication platform, taking about 30 minutes. The status is changed in the system, taking about 10 minutes, and then the closure team closes it, taking another 5 minutes. Incorrect status updates can cause confusion, which A fixes by reviewing logs and manually updating the status.

A coordinates these processes to ensure smooth operations and addresses issues like incorrect ticket information or missing documents promptly. In process optimization, A analyzes workflows in the three areas to identify inefficiencies and implements changes to streamline processes and enhance efficiency. The current focus is on understanding what usually goes wrong during process optimization.
